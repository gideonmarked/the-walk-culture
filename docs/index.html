StepQuest

Turn every step into treasure.

In development

StepQuest is a walking game for Android. The steps you already take are counted and converted into an in-game currency you can spend on cosmetics — outfits, pets, and home decor — while keeping your character healthy by staying active.

What it does

  • Walk to earnSteps become spendable currency, counted automatically from your phone's health data.
  • Stay healthyYour character's health level rises and falls with how much you walk each day.
  • Open Mystery SpheresHit step goals to unlock rewards — with the drop odds always shown up front.
  • Dress up & decorateHundreds of cosmetics, from common finds to a prestige tier worth more than gold.
  • Build a streakDaily quests and dozens of trophies to chase.
  • Friends & groupsAdd friends by code and team up in walking groups.

Privacy

Your step data is used to run the game and nothing else — it is never sold and never shared with advertisers. Read the full Privacy Policy.
