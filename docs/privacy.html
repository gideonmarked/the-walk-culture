The Walk Culture — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

The Walk Culture ("the app") turns the steps you walk into an in-game currency you can spend on cosmetic items. This policy explains exactly what the app reads, what it stores, and what it never does.

The short version. We read your daily step count. We use it to run the game. We never sell it, never send it to advertisers, and never use it to profile you. You can delete everything at any time.

Who we are

The Walk Culture is developed by Gideon Mark Pacete. For any privacy question or a data-deletion request, contact: gideonmarkcpacete@gmail.com.

What the app reads

DataSourceWhy
Daily step count Health Connect (Android) / HealthKit (iOS), with your permission The core of the game: steps become in-game currency, set your character's health level, and drive daily goals.
Live step count Your device's built-in step sensor (physical activity permission) So the on-screen counter ticks up while you walk. Read on the device only.
Game progress Created by the app Your wallet, items, streak, trophies and settings.
Anonymous account ID Created by the app A random identifier so your progress can be restored if you reinstall. It is not linked to your name, email, or phone number.
Purchase receipts Google Play, if you buy something To verify a purchase is genuine before unlocking what you paid for.

The app requests only one health data type — your step count — and only for the current day. It does not read your location, heart rate, sleep, weight, or any other health data.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. To anyone, ever.
  • We never send health or step data to advertisers, or use it to target ads.
  • We never share health data with third parties except the hosting provider that stores your game progress (below).
  • We do not track your location. No location permission is requested.
  • We do not require an account, an email address, or your real name to play.

Where your data is stored

Game progress is saved on your device. If cloud save is enabled, a copy is also stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider) so your progress survives reinstalling or changing phones. Data is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and is readable only by your own anonymous account.

Your raw health data is not uploaded. Only the resulting game state — such as your step totals for the day and your currency balance — is stored.

Your prayers and reflections stay on your device. Anything you write in the gratitude journal, and any name you enter when praying, is kept only on your phone and is never uploaded to our servers or shared. Uninstalling the app erases it.

Advertising

The Walk Culture may show optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for in-game currency. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may access your device's advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy for how it handles that data.

Your health and step data is never shared with AdMob or any advertiser. Watching ads is always optional — the game is fully playable without them.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card details. We receive only a purchase receipt, which we verify with Google in order to unlock what you bought.

Children

The Walk Culture is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Revoke health access at any time in Health Connect (Android) or Settings → Privacy → Health (iOS). The game keeps working; it just stops counting new steps.
  • Delete everything. Uninstalling removes all local data. To erase any cloud copy, email us and we will delete your account and all associated data.
  • Request a copy of your stored data by emailing us.

Data retention

Cloud-saved progress is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account removes it permanently. Purchase records may be retained where required for tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and note the change in the app before the change takes effect.
