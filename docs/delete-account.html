Delete your The Walk Culture account

The Walk Culture, by Gideon Mark Pacete · Last updated: 18 November 2026

You can delete your The Walk Culture account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open The Walk Culture.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

Option 2 — by email (if you no longer have the app)

  1. Email gideonmarkcpacete@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account.
  2. Include your account code (7 characters, shown on your Profile and on the Friends & Groups screen), your username, or the Google account email you linked, so we can find your account.

We delete the account and reply to confirm within 30 days.

What is kept

For each Pebble pack you bought, we keep only the Google Play purchase token and the product name, with no link to you or your account. This stops the same receipt being paid out twice. Your Google Play order history is held by Google, not by us.

Deleting your account does not cancel a VIP subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

See also our privacy policy.
