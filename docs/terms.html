The Walk Culture — Terms of Use

Last updated: 18 November 2026

These terms apply when you use The Walk Culture ("the app"). By using the app, and before you post anything other people can see, you agree to them.

The short version. Be kind. Don't post anything hateful, sexual, threatening, or that identifies a real person. Report anything that breaks these rules, and block anyone you don't want to hear from.

Content other people can see

Some things you create are visible to other players:

  • your username and account code,
  • the names of groups you create,
  • prayer requests you choose to share (shown anonymously).

Community rules

You must not post content that:

  • harasses, bullies, threatens, or intimidates anyone;
  • is hateful toward people because of race, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, nationality, sex, gender, sexual orientation, or similar traits;
  • is sexual or sexually suggestive, or involves minors in any harmful way;
  • promotes violence, self-harm, illegal activity, or dangerous behaviour;
  • includes anyone's personal details — full names, phone numbers, addresses, emails, or social media handles — including your own;
  • is spam, advertising, or impersonates another person or organization.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report a prayer request, a player, or a group from inside the app. Prayer requests are hidden automatically once several people report them.
  • Block a player to remove them as a friend, stop friend requests between you, and stop seeing their prayer requests.
  • We review reports and may remove content, or suspend or delete accounts, that break these rules — without notice.

Purchases

Pebble packs and VIP subscriptions are sold through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). VIP renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it: in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or on iPhone under Settings → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends. Purchased items are for use in the app only, have no cash value, and can't be transferred.

Health

The app is a game, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, and walk safely: watch where you're going, not your phone.

Your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time in Settings → Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. How we handle your data is described in our privacy policy.

Changes

If these terms change materially, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
